Elements in this example

<table> holds the data; <caption> names it; <colgroup> and <col> identify columns.

<thead>, <tbody>, and <tfoot> divide the table into sections. <tr> creates a row, <th> marks a heading, and <td> holds a data cell.

The rowspan and colspan attributes let cells span multiple rows or columns.
